## Evaluation Criteria (You must strictly adhere to these)

1. Novelty & Necessity: (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

  - Check for Duplication: Does the rule have a conceptual overlap with existing rules?

  - Judge the Operation: If there is an overlap, is the diff a REPLACE operation for consolidation and refinement? An ADD operation that introduces a redundant concept must be REJECTED.

  - Assess Value: Does this rule provide a genuinely valuable new insight?

2. Generalizability & Universality: Is the rule truly independent of the specific task logic? Can it be applied to a wide range of problems?

3. Evidence-Driven: Is the change strongly and clearly supported by the performance metrics and analysis in the report? Is the gain significant?

4. Clarity & Actionability:

  - Check for Contradiction: Does the rule contradict any existing rules?

  - Resolve Conflicts: If a potential contradiction exists, does its Apply condition clearly define the scope to resolve the conflict? If it leads to ambiguity, it must be REJECTED.

  - Clarity of Language: Are the rule's principle and application scenario stated clearly, concisely, and unambiguously?

5. Correct Formatting & Categorization: Does the diff correctly use the ## Category and ### Rule ID... structure? Is the placement of the new/modified rule under the correct category logical and justified based on the category descriptions?

6. Technical Correctness: Is the underlying computer science principle cited in the rule technically accurate?
