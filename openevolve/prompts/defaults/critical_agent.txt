## Evaluation Criteria (You must strictly adhere to these)

1. Generalizability & Universality: Is the rule truly independent of the specific task logic? Can it be applied to a wide range of problems? (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

2. Evidence-Driven: Is the change strongly and clearly supported by the performance metrics and analysis in the report? Is the gain significant?

3. Correct Formatting & Categorization: Does the diff correctly use the ## Category and ### Rule ID... structure? Is the placement of the new/modified rule under the correct category logical and justified? Does it follow the concise format precisely?

4. Clarity & Actionability: Is the rule's principle and application scenario stated clearly and concisely? Is it unambiguous?

5. Novelty & Necessity: Is this a genuinely new and valuable principle, or is it a trivial or redundant restatement of an existing rule?

6. Technical Correctness: Is the underlying computer science principle cited in the rule technically accurate?
